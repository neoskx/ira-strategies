## Description
Classic 60/40 portfolio: 60% Total US Market, 40% Total Bond Market. The traditional balanced portfolio for long-term investors seeking growth with reduced volatility.

## Logic
Set allocations and rebalance annually. Simple and time-tested.

## Rebalance frequency
Annual.

## Suitable for
- Min horizon years: 10
- Risk tolerance: moderate
- Max drawdown tolerance: -40%
