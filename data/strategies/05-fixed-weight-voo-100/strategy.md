## Description
100% S&P 500 via Vanguard VOO. The classic buy-and-hold benchmark. Most active strategies should beat this to justify their complexity.

## Logic
Set to 100% VOO. Annual rebalance to restore from fractional drift.

## Rebalance frequency
Annual.

## Suitable for
- Min horizon years: 15
- Risk tolerance: aggressive
- Max drawdown tolerance: -50%
